Overview

The E800 Series is a general-purpose vector inverter covering an applicable motor range of 0.2–450 kW across single-phase 220–240 V, three-phase 230 V and three-phase 380–480 V supplies. Combining advanced motor control with a compact footprint, it delivers V/F control and open-loop sensorless vector control for both induction motors and PMSM permanent magnet synchronous motors, producing 150% starting torque at just 0.5 Hz, a 1:100 speed control range and ±0.5% steady-state speed precision.

Built for real-world industrial automation, the E800 offers 15-stage speed control, built-in PID for process closed-loop regulation, auto voltage regulation, DC braking, speed-tracing restart, and dedicated multi-pump control with sleep/wake dormancy for constant-pressure water supply systems. Frequency reference and command channels are fully flexible: keypad, control terminals, analog signals or Modbus RTU over the built-in RS-485 port.

Housed in a compact wall-mounted format — flame-retardant polycarbonate up to 45 kW and powder-coated metal cabinets from 55–450 kW — the drive integrates a removable LED keypad, optional built-in braking unit, optional EMI filter and optional DC choke. Comprehensive protection functions and CE / UL certification make the E800 a dependable choice for continuous industrial duty.

Key Features

High-Performance Vector Control

V/F (VVVF), open-loop vector for induction motors and SVC for PMSM; 0.5 Hz/150% start torque, 1:100 speed range, ±0.5% steady speed precision.

Wide Power & Voltage Coverage

0.2–450 kW across 1-phase 220–240 V, 3-phase 230 V and 3-phase 380–480 V inputs; output up to 590 Hz.

Built-In PID & Multi-Pump Control

Process closed-loop control plus pump interchange logic with sleep/wake function for water-supply systems.

15-Stage Speed + Jog Control

Auto-circulating or terminal-controlled multi-speed running with 0.1–3000 s jog times.

Modbus RTU Communication

Built-in RS-485 with three command channels and four frequency sources plus five auxiliary sources.

Integrated Braking & Filtering

Built-in braking unit standard on 3-phase 400 V models ≤30 kW; optional C3 EMI filter and DC choke.

Robust Protection & Reliability

Phase loss, over/under-voltage, overcurrent, 120%/60 s overload, stall and overheat protection; IP20, −10 to +40 °C.

Certified Quality

CE (≤450 kW) and UL+CE (≤22 kW); designed to IEC/EN 61800-5-1 and 61800-3.
