Overview

The SD20-E is the third-generation bus-type servo drive, engineered as a high-performance drive with a 3.2 kHz frequency response — more than double the previous generation. Built on a dual-core processor with a hardware current loop, 6 kHz position loop and 16 kHz speed loop, it delivers smooth motor control with no vibration at standstill and precise, fast response across the widest automation tasks.

With built-in EtherCAT (CANopen available as an option), the SD20-E has passed the official EtherCAT Conformance Test (CTT), guaranteeing compatibility with PLCs and automation controllers from different brands. Bidirectional data transmission reaches 100 Mbps with cycle times down to 250 μs, using DC distributed-clock synchronization with sub-microsecond jitter. Feedback options include incremental encoders, absolute encoders up to 23-bit (38 million pulses/rev) and resolvers.

Commissioning is simplified by gain autotuning, inertia autotuning, online rotating inertia identification, model feedforward control and notch filters. Rich functionality includes full-closed-loop control, gantry synchronization, electronic CAM, a built-in PLC for simple user programs, multi-position control, moving fixed-length cutting, gain switching, homing search and vibration suppression — making the SD20-E a complete motion solution.

Key Features

3.2 kHz Frequency Response

Third-generation dual-core platform with hardware current loop, 6 kHz position loop and 16 kHz speed loop.

Built-in EtherCAT (CoE)

Passed the official EtherCAT Conformance Test for cross-brand PLC compatibility; 100 Mbps, DC distributed clock, min. cycle 250 μs, jitter < 1 μs.

CANopen Option

Fieldbus flexibility with CANopen (F4) or Modbus via RS485/RS232 and USB for setup and monitoring.

23-Bit Absolute Encoder Support

Up to 38 million pulses per revolution; also supports incremental encoders and resolvers.

7 Control Modes

Profile Position, Profile Velocity, Profile Torque, Homing, Cyclic Sync Position, Cyclic Sync Velocity, Cyclic Sync Torque.

Auto-Tuning & Feedforward

Gain autotuning, inertia autotuning, online inertia identification, model feedforward and notch filters for fast commissioning.

Built-in PLC & Electronic CAM

User-programmable logic plus E-CAM, gantry synchronization and multi-position control for complex motion.

Comprehensive Protection

Overvoltage/undervoltage, overload, overcurrent, overspeed, encoder error, phase loss, overheat, regen brake error, battery under-voltage and sync-loss protection.
