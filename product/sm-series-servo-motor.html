Overview

The SM Series permanent magnet synchronous servo motor is a high-performance motion solution, engineered to pair seamlessly with EURA DRIVES servo drives to form a complete, tightly integrated servo system. Built around high-energy rare-earth permanent magnets, the SM Series delivers exceptional torque density and efficiency in a compact, lightweight frame.

Advanced electromagnetic design minimizes torque ripple and cogging for smooth, stable operation even at low speeds, while the optimized low-inertia rotor provides the fast dynamic response demanded by high-acceleration positioning cycles. High-resolution encoder feedback — available in incremental or absolute versions — ensures precise speed and position control for exacting automation tasks.

With six flange sizes from 40 mm to 180 mm, each offered in standard and holding-brake versions, the SM Series covers a broad spectrum of automation loads. Rugged IP65 construction with shaft oil seal and Class F insulation guarantees a long, reliable service life even in harsh industrial environments.

Key Features

High-Energy Rare-Earth Magnets

Outstanding torque density in a compact, lightweight package for space-constrained machine designs.

Smooth Low-Speed Operation

Optimized electromagnetic design delivers low torque ripple and cogging for stable, precise motion.

Fast Dynamic Response

Low-inertia rotor with a high torque-to-inertia ratio for high-acceleration positioning cycles.

High-Precision Feedback

Wide Speed Range

Rugged Protection

Six Frame Sizes

Matched System Pairing

Designed to operate as a complete matched servo system with EURA DRIVES SD20/SDE20 series drives.
