Overview

The EP66 series is a high-protection-grade frequency inverter built for the harshest environments. With rugged IP66/NEMA 4 construction compliant with EN 60529, the drive is completely dust-tight and fully protected against jets of water and heavy waves — allowing wall-mounted, cabinet-free installation in locations where ordinary inverters cannot survive. A removable cable conduit plate with humidity-barrier vent keeps moisture out, while polycarbonate (≤15 kW) and metal (18.5–90 kW) housings deliver durable, corrosion-resistant performance from 0.4 kW to 90 kW.

At its heart, a DSP-based motor control platform supports V/F control, sensorless vector control and PMSM sensorless vector control in speed or torque modes, delivering 150% starting torque at just 0.5 Hz, a 1:100 speed control range and ±0.5% steady-state speed precision. Intelligent autotuning makes setup quick, and all standard inverter functions are built in — 15-stage speed control, built-in PID, auto voltage regulation, DC braking, speed-track starting and jog control.

Designed for real-world convenience, the EP66 offers space inside the drive for customer options such as a main/emergency switch, start/stop selectors, a potentiometer and a brake resistor, plus an optional built-in bypass switch. A C3-class EMC filter is standard. Smart PC tools support control and troubleshooting, and configurable LCD keypads are remote-mountable via waterproof connector. CE and UL certifications make it ready for the worldwide market.

Key Features

IP66/NEMA 4 Rugged Construction

Dust-tight and fully protected against powerful water jets (EN 60529); installs cabinet-free in harsh, wet and dusty locations.

Versatile Motor Control Platform

DSP-based control for V/F, sensorless vector (SVC) and PM synchronous motor control in speed or torque mode.

High Torque, High Precision

150% starting torque at 0.5 Hz, 100% rated torque at 5% rated speed (PMSM), 1:100 speed range, ±0.5% steady speed precision.

Intelligent Autotuning

Quick, automatic motor setup for easy commissioning.

Space for Built-In Options

Main/emergency switch, start/stop selectors, potentiometer, brake resistor and optional bypass switch inside the drive.

Standard EMC Filtering

C3-class filter as standard; optional built-in C1 filter for residential environments.

Comprehensive Built-In Functions

15-stage speed running, auto circulating, built-in PID, AVR, DC braking, speed-track startup, 150%/60 s overload.

Smart Connectivity & HMI

RS-485 Modbus (CAN option), remote-mountable 1-/4-line LCD keypads via waterproof connector, smart PC tools.
