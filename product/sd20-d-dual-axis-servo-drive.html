Overview

The SD20-D series is a high-integration dual-axis AC servo drive system that packs two complete servo drive axes into a single compact book-style unit. By sharing one power-stage housing and wiring harness for both axes, it dramatically cuts cabinet space, wiring labor and overall system cost compared with two standalone drives — an ideal motion solution for multi-axis machines where space and cost are tight.

Built on an advanced motor-control algorithm with high-resolution feedback support, the SD20-D delivers a 2.5 kHz speed response, ±0.01% speed fluctuation, precise speed/torque control and smooth low-speed operation. Each axis provides 5 DI + 3 DO programmable I/O, and the drive supports 2500-line incremental, 23-bit incremental and 17/23-bit absolute encoders as well as resolvers, with full-closed-loop control supported.

Available in EtherCAT-type, CANopen-type and pulse-type variants, the SD20-D covers position, speed and torque applications across a broad range of automated machinery. Advanced functions include gantry synchronization, electronic cam, a built-in PLC, high-speed counter, homing search, interrupt fixed-length positioning and auto gain tuning with online inertia identification.

Key Features

Dual-Axis Integration

Two axes in one unit, saving up to ~50% of installation space and reducing wiring cost versus two single-axis drives.

Compact Book-Type Design

203 × 84 × 196 mm shared chassis for dense cabinet mounting.

High Dynamic Performance

2.5 kHz speed response, 1:10,000 speed range, ±0.01% speed fluctuation, 1-command-unit positioning accuracy.

Fieldbus Variants

Dedicated EtherCAT (CoE, DC sync, <1 μs jitter) and CANopen (CiA-301/402) models, plus pulse type with Modbus.

Flexible Encoder Support

2500-line incremental, 23-bit incremental, 17/23-bit absolute (battery-backed multiturn), resolver; full closed-loop.

Per-Axis Programmable I/O

5 DI + 3 DO per axis (10 DI + 6 DO total), with cross-axis expansion.

Advanced Motion Functions

Gantry synchronization, electronic cam, built-in PLC, high-speed counter, interrupt fixed-length positioning.

Auto-Tuning

Automatic gain tuning with online inertia identification keeps commissioning fast as load characteristics vary.
