About FarFlight

A comprehensive trading enterprise connecting China's manufacturing strength with the global market.

FarFlight Trading Co., Ltd is a comprehensive trading enterprise with extensive experience in cross-border commerce. We have mature expertise in the domestic and international distribution and sales of machinery & electronics, household goods, and sports & fitness products.

Backed by a well-established global supply chain and sales network, our products are widely sold across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Africa. With reliable product quality, flexible trading solutions, efficient logistics, and professional service, we have built long-term partnerships and earned the trust of clients worldwide.

Our mechatronics division focuses on industrial motion control. We supply EURA DRIVES servo motors, servo drive systems and vector inverters — covering everything from compact 40 mm servo motors and slim single-board drives to 450 kW industrial inverters and IP67 motor-integrated units — helping machine builders, integrators and distributors worldwide keep their automation running.

Looking ahead, FarFlight Trading remains committed to the principles of integrity, quality, and mutual benefit — continuously expanding our product lines and global reach, and serving as a trusted bridge between manufacturers and international markets.
